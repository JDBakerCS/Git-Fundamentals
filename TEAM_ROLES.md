# Festival Team Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Release coordinator | Protects `main`, merges approved Pull Requests | Assigned to instructor |
| Schedule team | Updates times and locations | Ready |
| Experience team | Proposes attractions and visitor activities | Ready |
| Accessibility team | Reviews routes, formats, and descriptions | Ready |
| Review team | Checks accuracy, clarity, and scope of changes | Ready |

## Collaboration rule

Each task has one author and at least one reviewer. Authors explain what changed; reviewers verify the diff rather than approving from the title alone.

# Byte City Technology Festival Plan

## Festival goal

Create a welcoming one-day festival where visitors can explore technology through stories, demonstrations, games, and accessible hands-on activities.

## Confirmed schedule

- Opening ceremony: 10:00 AM at the Innovation Atrium
- Student demo hour: 11:00 AM in Lab 350
- Community design challenge: 1:00 PM in the Library Commons
- Closing showcase: 4:00 PM at the Rooftop Stage

## Attractions

- The Robot Poetry Booth
- The Human-vs-AI Drawing Wall
- The Debugging Escape Room
- The Accessible Navigation Challenge

## Accessibility plan

- Provide large-print and digital copies of the schedule.
- Keep primary routes free of temporary equipment.
- Provide quiet seating near each major venue.
- Ask presenters to describe important visual information aloud.

## Food and refreshments

- Water refill station
- Fruit and packaged snacks
- Vegetarian sandwiches

## Weather contingency

If the rooftop closes, the closing showcase will move to the Innovation Atrium.
